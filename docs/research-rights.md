# Ask Ace research source review

The public chat search uses only sources with confirmed permission for this use. The
current approved text includes the [Power Curve Working Group paper](https://wes.copernicus.org/articles/5/199/2020/) and three Wind Energy
Science papers on [wind-speed variability](https://wes.copernicus.org/articles/7/2255/2022/),
[turbulence and loads](https://wes.copernicus.org/articles/11/509/2026/), and
[turbine wakes](https://wes.copernicus.org/articles/6/961/2021/). Each publisher article
page states CC BY 4.0; answers should credit the authors, title and DOI. The [ACE dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/23114177)
is also CC BY 4.0, but it is used through the separate data integration rather than
the document search index.

The NREL reports and handbook remain outbound references. NREL's
[copyright notice](https://www.nrel.gov/disclaimer.html) says use of documents may
be subject to copyright; being freely downloadable does not establish permission
for this public document index. The Paul Gipe book's public-domain claim and the
two patent PDFs' reuse status still need rights-holder or legal confirmation.
Those items are excluded from public search even if an older deployment has their
passages in its database. The default curated ingestion command downloads only
approved items. No article or PDF is served to visitors by this app.

UK [copyright guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/exceptions-to-copyright)
limits the text and data mining exception to non-commercial research with lawful
access. Do not treat that exception as permission for a public product.
